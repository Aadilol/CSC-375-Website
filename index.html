About Our Organization

Campus Lockers Ltd. is the five-member systems analysis team behind this project, formed for CSC 375, "Introduction to System Analysis at the University of Victoria". Operating under the banner "Storage for All", the team analyzes, designs and prototypes a centralized locker registration system for UVic's self-claimed hallway lockers, while linking out to the university's existing bike, gym and Engineering Lab Wing locker services.

The team's work spans requirements gathering, systems analysis, data and process modelling, and UI/UX prototyping utilizing each member's background to deliver a tested, usable design by the end of the Fall 2026 term.

Our Client

The University of Victoria (UVic) is the client and sponsor for this project. As a public research university with more than 20,000 undergraduate and graduate students, UVic offers several kinds of on-campus storage, but each is run by a different department with its own process and no shared source of information.

UVic's interest in this project reflects its broader commitment to student-friendly campus services: a centralized locker system would reduce confusion, cut down on locker-related inquiries to Campus Security, CARSA and the ECSS, and give the university a tested design it can build on in the future.

Project Description

Executive Summary, adapted from the Project Charter (v2.2).

University of Victoria students currently navigate four disconnected ways to find and secure a locker: self-claimed hallway lockers with no records, bike lockers rented through Campus Security, gym lockers booked through CARSA, and Engineering Lab Wing lockers registered through an online portal. Campus Lockers addresses this by designing a centralized, mobile-friendly web application that maps every known locker location on campus, lets students register, renew and release self-claimed hallway lockers, and links directly to the existing registration processes for bike, gym and ELW lockers.

The project covers analysis, design and prototyping only with a usability-tested UI prototype, a deployed system running from September to December 2026.
